# Bradley W. Baker, Ph.D.

Earth Scientist & Geospatial Analyst

I am an Earth scientist and geospatial analyst with experience in environmental GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, geospatial data management, and data quality/validation. My professional background includes nearly six years supporting NASA Earth science programs, along with research in climatology, severe weather, climate variability, environmental hazards and land use change.

## Areas of Focus

- Spatial Statistics
- Environmental & Hazard Analysis
- Geospatial Data Management & QA/QC
- Python & Geospatial Programming
- Scientific Data Processing
